Your phone can wait. Your exam can’t.

LockedIn is an Android app for students. Pick how long you want to focus, start the session, and the apps that pull you away stay out of reach until the timer ends.

Ready

    Three steps, no setup ritual

    Starting should take seconds, because a focus tool you avoid opening doesn’t help anyone.

    Choose a duration

    Set the time you want to be left alone, from a quick revision burst to a full study block.

    Start the session

    Distracting apps are restricted while the countdown runs, and you can always see how much time is left.

    Stay reachable

    Incoming calls are meant to keep working, so focusing never means being unreachable for something important.

    What’s built, and what isn’t yet

    LockedIn is an early prototype. Here is where things honestly stand.

    Prototype built

    Timed focus sessions

    A native Android app where you pick a duration and start a session. Tried by more than 10 early testers so far.

    Being tested

    App restriction

    The core behaviour. We’re testing it across Android versions and phone makers before promising anything absolute.

    Planned

    AI study assistant

    Explanations and revision help grounded in credible, properly licensed academic sources, with references so you can check them.

    Planned

    Study groups

    Shared focus sessions and peer accountability, so you and your friends study together.

    Planned

    Learning repository

    One place to keep and find your notes, materials and resources.

    Where we’re headed
